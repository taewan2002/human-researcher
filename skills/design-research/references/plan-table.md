| Period | Person-hour cap | Work and allocated resource use | Sum of allocations | Work to cut or defer if it overruns |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| … | … | Preparation … + active work … + analysis/review … | … ≤ period cap | … |
| Total | … | All periods | … ≤ total cap | … |

Check every sum before delivering the schedule. An uncertain duration range is not an allocation that fits the cap: when its upper end exceeds available time, specify the narrower scope or a stop/defer rule. Check units, multiplicities, and all resources occupied by the same activity; distinguish aggregate quantities from per-item requirements. Waiting consumes effort only when attendance is required, but can still constrain the calendar. Keep uncertain quantities conditional rather than silently changing the total.

Reading and implementation are dependencies; milestones should reveal what the researcher can decide next.
