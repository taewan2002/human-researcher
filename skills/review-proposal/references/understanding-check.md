# Plain-language understanding check

Use only when asked to assess the researcher's understanding. An ordinary document review needs no interview, and fluent prose does not establish its author's understanding.

Choose questions from the study's purpose, closest comparison, reasoning, feasibility, and evidence that would change a decision. Adapt to the actual study and requested focus; use plain language and the requested count. Each item asks one focused question, without hidden subquestions. Replace questions rather than append a second interview.

Assess understanding only after the researcher supplies their own explanation. Do not answer on their behalf or count model answers as demonstrated understanding.
