## Paper links

Use the actual URL in the supplied material or lookup output, preserving version information. After fetch_arxiv.py, use `metadata.url`; after forward_citations.py, use returned paper URLs, distinguishing originals from index-record links. Do not turn a title or acronym into an identifier. For an absent URL, write **링크 미확인 — 제공 파일 P01.md** / **Link unverified — supplied file P01.md**.

Show recognizable titles for both ends of a citation edge, including the seed; IDs and author names alone can be ambiguous. Explain the supported claim and actual inspected location. A URL's presence does not establish identity, full-text access, or claim validity; preserve synthetic source labels. Author/lab links need identity and affiliation evidence.
