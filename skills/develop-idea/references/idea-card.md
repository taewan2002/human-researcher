# Idea card

Use the parts needed for the requested idea or critique; this is not a mandatory questionnaire.

**Selected direction and research question:** …
**Candidate hypothesis / answer:** …
**Purpose in plain language:** who needs what change or knowledge, and why existing approaches are insufficient.

| Closest work: title, original link, inspected evidence | Proposed difference | Why it could matter | What remains unverified |
|---|---|---|---|
| … | … | … | … |

For a visual idea card, connect the existing approach → proposed change → observation that would matter; identify what stays fixed and label the change as a hypothesis or plan. Use only known source metadata and links.

**Strongest alternative explanation:** …
**Smallest observation that distinguishes it:** …
**Pursuit estimate:** evidence for the need and tractability; hardest assumption; resources and cost of the first informative check.
**Keep / narrow / drop this idea if:** …

A critique-only request can stop at the objection, its basis, and a discriminating check.
